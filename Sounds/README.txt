Put your own sound files into this folder, one per trigger, as .mp3 or .ogg:

  crit.mp3            critical hit
  herb.mp3            herb gathered
  ping_attack.mp3     ping "Attack"
  ping_warning.mp3    ping "Warning"
  ping_assist.mp3     ping "Assist"
  ping_onmyway.mp3    ping "On my way"
  ping_threat.mp3     ping "Threatening alert"
  ping_nonthreat.mp3  ping "Look here" (also any ping during instance combat lockdown)
  ping_action.mp3     spell or item ping ("... is ready!", "Can't cast ...")

The ping files replace the game's ping sounds.
Restart the game after adding new files; /reload does not find them.
Back up this folder before updating the addon, an update may replace it.
